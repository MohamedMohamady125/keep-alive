What it does

Small, quiet, and it stays out of your way

Moves the mouse

Every 20 seconds the cursor slides 5 px and comes right back. You won't lose your place.

Scrolls now and then

Every third nudge it scrolls down a little and back up, so apps that watch scrolling see activity too.

Stops when you say

Click Stop, close the window, or set a timer like 60 min and it turns itself off.

Setup · about a minute

How to start using it

You only do this once. After that, open the app and press Start.

  1. 1
    Download and unzip

    Double-click KeepAlive-mac.zip in your Downloads folder.

  2. 2
    Move it to Applications

    Drag Keep Alive into your Applications folder.

  3. 3
    Open it the first time with a right-click

    Right-click Keep Alive, choose Open, then click Open again. Double-clicking won't work the first time.

  4. 4
    Allow it to move the mouse

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on Keep Alive.

  5. 5
    Reopen and press Start

    Quit the app, open it again, and click Start. Leave the small window open; minimizing it is fine.

Questions

Good to know

Why does my computer show a warning?

The app isn't registered with Apple or Microsoft yet, so they show a caution the first time. It doesn't collect or send anything.

Will it mess with what I'm doing?

The cursor moves a few pixels and returns. Scrolling happens in whatever is under the mouse, so leave it over the desktop or a blank area.

Does it work on older Macs?

This version is for Macs with Apple chips (M1 and newer). Ask the person who sent you this page if you have an Intel Mac.

How do I turn it off?

Click Stop, or close the window. Nothing keeps running in the background after that.
